Free Printable AAC Board Templates for Classrooms & Routines (PDF)

Low-tech physical paper AAC communication boards provide an essential backup for pool play, outdoor trips, messy therapy activities, and classroom environments. Discover starter board layouts and learn how seamlessly physical paper and digital AAC apps work together.

Why Every AAC User Needs Paper Backups alongside Digital Devices

High-tech speech-generating devices and tablets offer wonderful voice output, but physical paper communication boards remain vital. Speech-Language Pathologists (SLPs) always recommend keeping laminated low-tech paper boards on hand for scenarios where electronics aren't suitable:

  • Water & Outdoor Activities: Bath time, swimming pools, splash pads, and rainy playground trips.
  • High-Mess Environments: Finger painting, baking, sensory play, and mealtimes.
  • Low Battery & Travel Emergencies: Long car rides or school days when a device loses charge.

Essential Printable Starter Layouts for Home & School

Organizing paper boards with high-contrast symbol tiles and consistent core word positioning helps nonverbal communicators quickly locate words. Popular starter layout templates include:

  • Mealtime & Kitchen Layouts: Visual grids featuring actionable core cues like Hungry, Thirsty, More, All Done, Like, Dislike, Eat, Drink alongside common food choices.
  • Emotional Regulation & Feelings Boards: High-contrast boards displaying core emotional state tiles like Happy, Sad, Tired, Hurt, Scared, Overwhelmed, Need Space to support quick self-regulation.
  • Daily Classroom & Routine Schedules: Sequential visual schedules for classroom transitions, morning routines (Arrive, Backpack, Desk, Circle Time), and hygiene steps.
